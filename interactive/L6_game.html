You or the network? Sentiment of financial news

You read 10 real sentences from financial news about listed companies and decide whether each one is good news, bad news or neither for an investor in the company. After each answer you see the label given by the network of Lecture 6 and the label given by the annotators of the data set (people with a background in financial markets).

The network is the embedding bag of the lecture: it averages the vectors of the words and word pairs of a sentence, starting from the word2vec vectors learned on speeches of the European Central Bank (ECB), and passes the average to a softmax layer. It was trained on 3,385 other sentences and never saw these ones; on the 727 test sentences of this split it is right in 72.4% of cases. In the data most sentences are neutral (59.3%); the game shows more positive and negative ones.

Class board for the projector

Data: Financial PhraseBank (Malo et al., 2014; 4,838 sentences, 5–8 annotators each). The 40 sentences of the game are drawn at random from the test sentences of the first of the five random splits of the lecture (12 negative, 12 positive, 16 neutral, at most 40 words; seed 20261108); every game draws 10 of them. Predictions: cached outputs of analysis_L6.py. Code: lectures/L6/make_game_L6.py.
