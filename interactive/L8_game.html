Real or generated? S&P 500 returns

Each card shows two series of 250 daily returns of the S&P 500 stock index, about one trading year. One is real, from the years 2005–2019; the other was produced by the generative adversarial network (GAN) of Lecture 8, trained on the same years. Pick the real one.

The model you play against is not a network but a statistical check from the lecture: volatility clustering. For each series it computes the average autocorrelation of the absolute returns over lags 1 to 5 (one trading week) and calls the series with the larger value real.

Class board for the projector

Data: S&P 500, daily adjusted closing prices (Yahoo Finance), log returns 4 January 2005 – 31 December 2019; GAN samples: lectures/L8/cache/gan.npz (generator of seed 1). The 24 pairs of the game are drawn at random (seed 20261108); every game shows 10 of them, in random order and position. Code: lectures/L8/make_game_L8.py.
