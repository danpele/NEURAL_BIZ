Five rounds on how attention works and what it is worth. In each round you first predict what will happen and lock your answer, then test it on the course data, and only then see the proof. Rounds 1 and 2 draw the real attention of FinBERT on finance tweets as a 3D landscape you can turn; rounds 3 to 5 use the 500 test tweets and the models of the lecture.

Charts stay empty until you lock a prediction. In rounds 1 and 2, drag the landscape to turn it; scroll or pinch to zoom.
