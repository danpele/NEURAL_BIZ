You or the network? A credit game

You are the credit officer of a bank. You see 10 real home-equity loan applications and decide: approve or refuse. After each decision you see what the network of Lecture 1 decided and whether the client repaid. In the full data 19.9% of the loans defaulted; in the game one application in three is a default.

The network is the multilayer perceptron (MLP 64-64) of the lecture, trained on 3,576 other loans; it never saw these applications. It refuses a loan when its probability of default is at least 50%.

Class board for the projector

Data: HMEQ home-equity loans (Baesens, Roesch and Scheule, 2016; 5,960 loans). The 36 applications of the game are drawn at random from the test loans (12 defaults, 24 repaid; seed 20261108); every game draws 10 of them. Code: lectures/L1/make_game_L1.py.
