# Log of AI Errors

List at least **three** places where an AI (artificial intelligence) assistant was wrong during the project,
and how you found out. Errors that you caught are a strength of the project, not a weakness.

Typical errors in deep-learning projects: a wrong formula or loss; data leakage (scaling, imputation or
feature selection fitted on the whole sample, or a random split of a time series, so the test period leaks into
training); look-ahead bias in financial or energy series; accuracy reported on a highly imbalanced target
(for example fraud) instead of precision, recall or the area under the precision-recall curve; results that
depend on one random seed; a pretrained model or tokenizer that does not match the one described; a sign or
unit error; data, numbers or model outputs that were invented; a reference that does not exist.
